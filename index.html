Delete your Pinpon account

Pinpon (com.pinpon.mobile)

You can request the deletion of your Pinpon account and its associated data at any time. Account deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.

How to delete your account

  1. Open the Pinpon app and sign in.
  2. Tap your profile picture in the top corner to open your profile.
  3. Select Delete Account.
  4. Review the warning and tap Delete to confirm.

If you cannot access the app, you can also request deletion by emailing us at support@example.com from the email address linked to your account. We will process your request within 30 days.

What happens to your data

When you delete your account, the following data is permanently removed:

  • Your profile information (name, email address, phone number, address)
  • Your saved partners and favorites
  • Your booking and redemption history

Some data may be retained where required by law or for legitimate business purposes — for example, transaction records required for accounting or fraud prevention — for no longer than legally permitted, and is not used for any other purpose.

Please note: Deleting your account is irreversible. You will lose access to all services, saved partners, and history associated with your account.

Need help?

Contact us at support@example.com if you have questions about deleting your account or your data.
