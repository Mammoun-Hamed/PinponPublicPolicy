Privacy Policy

Pinpon (com.pinpon.mobile) · Last updated: October 2025

This privacy policy explains how Pinpon ("the app", "we", "us") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our mobile application. By using the app, you agree to the practices described below.

Information we collect

  • Account information — your name, email address, phone number, country, city, and address, provided when you create an account.
  • Location data — your device location, used to show nearby partners and offers. Location is only accessed while you use the app and with your permission.
  • Device information — device model and operating system details used for authentication and troubleshooting.
  • Usage data — your bookings, redemptions, saved partners, and in-app activity needed to provide the service.

Permissions the app uses

  • Location — to display partners and offers near you.
  • Camera — to scan QR codes for redeeming offers (partners).
  • Microphone — for optional voice search. Audio is processed only when you activate it.
  • Internet — to communicate with our servers.

How we use your information

  • To create and manage your account.
  • To show you nearby partners, offers, and services.
  • To process bookings and QR-code redemptions.
  • To send account-related communications (e.g., email confirmation).
  • To improve and secure the app.

Data sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We share limited information only where necessary to provide the service — for example, confirming a redemption with the partner you are visiting — or where required by law.

Data retention and deletion

We retain your account data for as long as your account is active. You can delete your account at any time from your profile inside the app (Profile → Delete Account), which permanently removes your personal data, or by contacting us at the email below. Some records may be retained where required by law or for legitimate business purposes (such as transaction records), for no longer than legally permitted.

Security

We use industry-standard measures to protect your data, including encrypted connections between the app and our servers. However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

Children's privacy

The app is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.

Contact us

If you have questions about this policy or your data, contact us at pinpon.it.com.
